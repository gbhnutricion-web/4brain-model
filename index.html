How it is simulated. With linear hepatic elimination, paracetamol makes the model a linear system of 30 states. The page advances minute by minute with the exact transition matrix, x ← eA·Δt x + G·rate, computed in R from the same equations as the toolkit. It does not approximate: on opening, it replays the reference regimen and compares it with rxode2 (the seal above).

What you see. Blood runs as a continuous stream through the side bars — venous on the left, rising to the lung, and arterial on the right, distributing — and through the vessels of each organ. Its brightness is proportional to the drug concentration in that blood, on a linear scale whose maximum is the arterial peak of the regimen. Each organ fills from left to right in proportion to the milligrams it holds, on the same scale for all (full = the most any organ holds during the regimen), with the figure in mg inside. Lights remain for what is not blood: the gut, absorption, elimination and, in the Brain tab, the barriers and the CSF, at a rate that grows with the logarithm of their flow.

Independent re-implementation of the 4Brain model (Gaohua et al. 2016, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) nested in a reference whole-body physiology. It is a typical virtual subject: not a clinical regimen and not a dose recommendation. It is not the Simcyp Simulator and does not replace it.
